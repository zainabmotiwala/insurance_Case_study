CRITICAL ANALYTICAL CAVEAT:
An E20-labelled registration in VAHAN indicates that the vehicle's engine was
type-certified as E20 material-compliant at registration. It does not constitute
proof of actual E20 fuel dispensed or consumed, as retail petrol distribution
across pumps is determined by OMC blending infrastructure rather than vehicle
model tags.


5. ELECTRIC & HYBRID VEHICLE TRENDS WITH CATEGORY DEFINITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORMAL CATEGORY DEFINITIONS IN VAHAN:
- Pure EV: Dedicated battery electric vehicle (BEV) propelled exclusively by
  electric motor(s) powered by an onboard traction battery pack, with external plug-in charging.
- Electric-BOV: Battery Operated Vehicle under Rule 2(u) of CMVR. In earlier VAHAN
  reporting, all EVs (low-speed 2W, electric 3W, early cars) were recorded under this label.
  From 2024, newer registrations transitioned primarily to Pure EV.
- Strong Hybrid EV (SHEV): Full hybrid containing combustion engine and electric motor
  with battery capable of pure electric propulsion at low speeds without plug-in (self-charging).
- Plug-in Hybrid EV (PHEV): Hybrid containing combustion engine and rechargeable battery
  pack capable of external mains charging and extended zero-emission range.
- Petrol-Hybrid / Petrol-E20-Hybrid / Diesel-Hybrid: Integrated Starter Generator (ISG)
  or mild-hybrid architectures providing torque assist and regenerative braking.

6. STATIONARITY TESTS (ADF & KPSS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Hypothesis Framework:
- Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF): H0 = Unit root present (Non-Stationary). Reject if p < 0.05.
- Kwiatkowski-Phillips-Schmidt-Shin (KPSS): H0 = Series is Level Stationary. Reject if p < 0.05.
- Series is confirmed stationary when ADF rejects H0 (p < 0.05) and KPSS fails to reject H0 (p >= 0.05).

8. DATA LIMITATIONS & CATEGORY EVOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Zero Missing Observations: The monthly series from 201701 to 202609 is unbroken
   (117 continuous monthly observations). No synthetic imputation or missing-value
   interpolation was performed or required.
2. Category Splitting in VAHAN:
   - PETROL(E20) was introduced in VAHAN records in February 2023. In earlier years,
     it appeared only in isolated records (1-3 vehicles) representing prototype test certifications.
   - Pure EV appeared as a distinct reporting code in 2022; earlier electric models
     were categorized as ELECTRIC(BOV). For historical electric analysis, Pure EV and
     Electric-BOV must be summed to reflect the true electric adoption path.
3. Absence of Vehicle-Class Segregation: VAHAN fuel-type reports aggregate all vehicle
   classes into single rows. Consequently, two-wheelers, three-wheelers, passenger cars,
   light commercial vehicles, and heavy trucks cannot be separated.
4. Administrative Tagging vs. Fuel Consumed: E20 figures track vehicle engine compliance
   certificates issued at registration, not the chemical blend percentage of fuel
   pumped from OMC retail dispensers.
